# The pentagonal hexecontahedron is a Nopert (Lean)

A *Nopert* is a polyhedron that is not Rupert (`¬ IsRupert`). The main theorems are in `Statement.lean`:

- `polarDualSnubDodecahedron_not_rupert`: the polar dual of Wikipedia's snub dodecahedron (`pentagonalHexecontahedron`, the poles of its facet planes) is a Nopert.
- `pentagonalHexecontahedron_not_rupert`: so is every similar copy (`IsPentagonalHexecontahedron`).
- `pentagonalHexecontahedron_eq`: that set is exactly 92 points, the I-orbits of three poles (so `pentagonalHexecontahedron_finite`).

The first two take as hypothesis the statement that no `IModel` is Rupert. The executable `constructPentagonalHexecontahedron` (repository root) checks the certificate packs natively and constructs that statement. The statement's own proof is kernel-checked (axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The C++ pipeline that produces the packs, and the instructions to reproduce everything, are in the companion repository; see its `README.md`.

## Files

- **Definition and bridge.**
  - `WikipediaSnub.lean`: Wikipedia's snub dodecahedron (ξ, p, M₁, M₂, the orbit) and the exact rotation from its frame to the 5-fold frame.
  - `PolarDual.lean`: `facetPoles`, Cramer's rule and the uniqueness of a pole, the two-sided-witness contradiction, affine independence, and invariance under orthogonal maps.
  - `Statement.lean`: `pentagonalHexecontahedron` and `IsPentagonalHexecontahedron`; the proof that the facet poles are the rotated base poles (decided interval checks, the pair certificates in integer arithmetic scaled by 2⁶⁴); the poles as an `IModel` (`phIModel`); the main theorems.
  - `PHStatementData.lean`: generated by `scripts/ph_statement_data.py`.
- **Model.**
  - `Vertices.lean`: the rational model (100 slots: the two z-axis vertices are listed once per fivefold orbit) and `C5Model`.
  - `IcoModel.lean`: `IModel`, three I-orbits (stabilizers C₅, C₃ and trivial) near the rational model, and the I-invariance of its hull.
  - `IcoInstance.lean`: `IModel.ofBoxes`, and the lemma that similarities preserve Rupert-ness.
- **Exact icosahedral group.**
  - `IcoField.lean`: K = ℚ(√5, sin 72°) in integer coordinates.
  - `IcoGroupData.lean`: generated by `scripts/snub_ico_group.py` (the group, the orbits of the model's slots, and Wikipedia's group).
  - `IcoGroup.lean`: closure, SO(3), Rz ∈ I.
- **Symmetry reductions.**
  - `IcoView.lean`: the view into the triangle T.
  - `AtlasIcoPrune.lean`: the icosahedral Dirichlet cell and its checked prune.
  - `IcoReduction.lean`: the relative rotation into that cell.
  - `Tightening.lean` and `FundamentalDomain.lean`: the fivefold pieces they build on.
- **Certificates.**
  - `Atlas*.lean`, `Certificate.lean`, `LocalCertificate.lean`, `SymmetryLocal.lean` and `SparseLocalViewTree.lean`: the certificate kinds and their soundness. The identity tube's rows are checked against every vertex.
- **Solution trees.**
  - `AtlasProjectiveSolutionTree.lean`: rows, validity and `valid_imp_noRupert_ix`.
  - `Packed*.lean`: the pack decoders.
  - `WedgeCover.lean` and `WedgeCoverData.lean`: the cover of T by the code triangles, the latter generated by `scripts/emit_wedge_cover.py` and checked natively.
- **Top level.**
  - `IsNotRupert.lean`: from valid tables to `¬ IsRupert`.
  - `NativeExecutable.lean`: the native checker.

The reduction chain is:

1. `IModel.exists_ico_full_atlas_translated_pose` reduces the view and the relative rotation.
2. `AtlasProjectiveSolutionTree.no_matrixPose_of_valid_tables` turns valid tables into the absence of a Rupert pose.
3. `IsNotRupert.not_rupert_of_valid_tables` gives `¬ IsRupert`.
4. `Statement.pentagonalHexecontahedron_not_rupert` concludes for every pentagonal hexecontahedron.
