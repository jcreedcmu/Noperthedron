# The snub dodecahedron is not Rupert (Lean)

The main theorems are in `Statement.lean`:

- `wikipediaSnubDodecahedron_not_rupert`: Wikipedia's snub dodecahedron (`snubDodecahedron`) is not Rupert.
- `snubDodecahedron_not_rupert`: neither is any similar copy (`IsSnubDodecahedron`).

Both take as hypothesis the statement that no `IModel` is Rupert. The executable `constructSnubDodecahedron` (repository root) checks the certificate packs natively and constructs that statement. The C++ pipeline that produces the packs, and the instructions to reproduce everything, are in the companion repository; see its `README.md`.

## Files

- **Definition and bridge.** `Statement.lean` has three parts:
  - ξ, p, M₁, M₂ and the orbit;
  - `IsSnubDodecahedron`;
  - an exact rotation from Wikipedia's frame to the 5-fold frame, a rational enclosure of the scaled vertex, and the decided check that its orbit is an `IModel`.
- **Model.**
  - `Vertices.lean`: the rational model and `C5Model`.
  - `IcoModel.lean`: `IModel`, an I-orbit near the rational model.
  - `IcoInstance.lean`: `IModel.ofBox`, and the lemma that similarities preserve Rupert-ness.
- **Exact icosahedral group.**
  - `IcoField.lean`: K = ℚ(√5, sin 72°) in integer coordinates.
  - `IcoGroupData.lean`: generated by `scripts/snub_ico_group.py`.
  - `IcoGroup.lean`: closure, SO(3), Rz ∈ I.
- **Symmetry reductions.**
  - `IcoView.lean`: the view into the triangle T.
  - `AtlasIcoPrune.lean`: the icosahedral Dirichlet cell and its checked prune.
  - `IcoReduction.lean`: the relative rotation into that cell.
  - `Tightening.lean` and `FundamentalDomain.lean`: the fivefold pieces they build on.
- **Certificates.**
  - `Atlas*.lean`, `Certificate.lean`, `LocalCertificate.lean`, `SymmetryLocal.lean` and `Sparse*.lean`: the certificate kinds and their soundness.
  - `GeneratedTangentCones.lean`: generated by `scripts/emit_tangent_cones.py`.
- **Solution trees.**
  - `AtlasProjectiveSolutionTree.lean`: rows, validity and `valid_imp_noRupert_ix`.
  - `Packed*.lean`: the pack decoders.
  - `WedgeCover.lean` and `WedgeCoverData.lean`: the cover of T by the code triangles, the latter generated by `scripts/emit_wedge_cover.py` and checked natively.
- **Top level.**
  - `IsNotRupert.lean`: from valid tables to `¬ IsRupert`.
  - `NativeExecutable.lean`: the native checker.

The reduction chain is:

1. `IModel.exists_ico_full_atlas_translated_pose` reduces the view and the relative rotation.
2. `AtlasProjectiveSolutionTree.no_matrixPose_of_valid_tables` turns valid tables into the absence of a Rupert pose.
3. `IsNotRupert.not_rupert_of_valid_tables` gives `¬ IsRupert`.
4. `Statement.snubDodecahedron_not_rupert` concludes for every snub dodecahedron.
